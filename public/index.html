Trading Rules
Read before you trade.

This website is a listing board only. No payments or items are ever exchanged here — every trade is completed in-game, directly between the two players.

Before you trade
  • Check the trader's vouches and scam-vouches before agreeing to anything.
  • Never send items or money first to someone with no vouches or with scam-vouches.
  • Consider using a trusted middleman for high-value trades.
  • Only vouch or scam-vouch traders you have actually dealt with.
Scamming is not tolerated

Scamming another player — taking their items or currency and not delivering what was agreed — is strictly against the rules. Reported and confirmed scammers may be permanently banned from using this marketplace.

Trade at your own risk. The marketplace owner is not responsible for losses from in-game trades between players.
